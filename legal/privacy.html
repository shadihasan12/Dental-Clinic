1. Who we are

Runbit ("Runbit", "we", "us") develops and operates Runbit Dental, a practice-management application that lets dental clinics manage patients, appointments, treatment plans, dental charts, invoices and staff.

For questions about this policy or about your data, contact us at support@runbit.tech.

2. Two kinds of data, two different roles

Runbit Dental handles two categories of information, and our responsibilities differ for each. This distinction matters for your rights and for who you should contact.

CategoryWhose dataOur role
Account & clinic data The dentists, assistants and administrators who log in to the app Controller. We decide how this data is used and this policy governs it directly.
Patient records The clinic's patients Processor. The clinic is the controller. We store and process patient records only on the clinic's instructions, and we do not use them for our own purposes.

If you are a patient of a clinic that uses Runbit Dental, your relationship is with that clinic. Direct requests to access, correct or delete your records to the clinic that treats you; they can action them in the app. We will assist the clinic where required.

3. What we collect

3.1 Account information

When a clinic user registers or is invited, we collect their name, email address, password (stored only as a salted hash — we never store or can read your plaintext password), role within the clinic, profile photo if uploaded, and the clinic and location they belong to.

3.2 Clinic information

Clinic name, address, working days and holidays, staff list and roles, and subscription plan.

3.3 Patient records entered by clinic staff

Clinic users enter patient information into the app, which may include patient name and contact details, dental charts and selected teeth, treatment types and plans, clinical notes, treatment status and dates, file attachments such as X-rays and clinical photographs, and costs, lab fees and payment records.

This is health data — a special category of personal data under GDPR Article 9 and comparable laws. We apply the protections in section 9 to it and process it only on the clinic's documented instructions. Clinics are responsible for having a lawful basis and any patient consent required in their jurisdiction before entering this data.

3.4 Billing information

Subscription plan, billing cycle, invoices and payment status. Card and payment-instrument details are handled by our payment provider and are never stored on our servers or on your device.

3.5 Technical and device information

To operate and secure the service we process a device push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send you notifications, app version, device model and operating system version, IP address and timestamps of requests, and error and crash diagnostics.

3.6 What we do not collect

  • We do not collect precise or background location.
  • We do not access your contacts, calendar, microphone or camera roll beyond files you explicitly pick.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not sell or share personal data for advertising or cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • We do not use patient records to train machine-learning models.

4. How we use it

  • Provide the service — authenticate you, sync your clinic's records across devices, and render charts, appointments and invoices.
  • Send notifications — appointment reminders and clinic activity alerts. You can turn these off in the app or in your device settings.
  • Billing — manage subscriptions, trials and invoices.
  • Support — respond to your requests through the in-app contact form.
  • Security and integrity — detect abuse, debug failures and maintain audit trails of changes to records.
  • Legal compliance — meet obligations that apply to us.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Legal bases (GDPR)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases for account and clinic data:

  • Contract — to provide the service you or your clinic subscribed to.
  • Legitimate interests — to secure the service, prevent abuse and improve reliability, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — to retain financial records and respond to lawful requests.
  • Consent — for push notifications and access to photos on your device, which you can withdraw at any time.

For patient records we act on the clinic's instructions; the clinic establishes the legal basis, typically Article 9(2)(h) (provision of health care) together with applicable national health-data law.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account data — for as long as the account is active, then deleted within 30 days of a deletion request.
  • Patient records — for as long as the clinic's subscription is active, and afterwards according to the clinic's instructions and any statutory medical-record retention period that applies to it. Clinics commonly must retain dental records for several years; we follow the clinic's direction here.
  • Invoices and financial records — as required by applicable tax and accounting law, typically up to 7 years, even after account deletion.
  • Diagnostic logs — up to 90 days.
  • Backups — deleted data persists in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before being overwritten.

9. How we protect it

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with TLS.
  • Data is encrypted at rest on our servers.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • Access is restricted by role — clinic staff see only the records their role permits, and admin-only areas are gated separately.
  • Changes to treatment records are recorded in an audit trail.
  • Access to production systems by our personnel is limited to those who need it and is logged.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data and poses a risk to your rights, we will notify you and the competent supervisory authority without undue delay and, where required, within 72 hours.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your data, correct inaccurate data, delete your data, restrict or object to processing, receive your data in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these, email support@runbit.tech from the address on your account. We respond within 30 days. You will not be charged, and we will not treat you differently for exercising a right.

If you are in the EEA or UK and believe we have not handled your data properly, you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are in California, you may exercise the rights described above under the CCPA/CPRA; note that we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined.

13. Children

Runbit Dental is a professional tool for dental practices and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under 18. Patient records may relate to child patients — those records are entered and controlled by the treating clinic under its own legal basis and parental-consent obligations, not by us.
