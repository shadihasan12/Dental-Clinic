Delete your account and data

Last updated: 23 July 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Runbit Dental account, what gets deleted, and what we are required to keep.

How to request deletion

Email support@runbit.tech from the email address registered on your account, with the subject "Account deletion request". Include the clinic name on the account.

We verify the request against the registered email, then confirm by reply. If you cannot email from the registered address, we will ask for other information to verify you own the account before acting.

What happens next

  • We confirm receipt within 5 business days.
  • Deletion is completed within 30 days of verification.
  • Deleted data is purged from encrypted backups within a further 30 days.

If you are a clinic administrator, deleting your account can affect the whole clinic. Tell us in your email whether you want (a) only your own staff account removed, or (b) the entire clinic workspace and all its records deleted. We will not delete a clinic workspace without explicit confirmation.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, password hash, phone number and profile photo.
  • Your role, permissions and working-hours settings.
  • Your device push tokens and notification preferences.
  • Where you request full workspace deletion: clinic details, patients, appointments, treatment plans, dental charts, clinical notes and file attachments.

Export your data first

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you need a copy of your clinic's records, request an export in the same email before we begin deletion, and we will provide it in a machine-readable format.

Patients of a clinic

If you are a patient rather than a clinic user, contact the dental clinic that treats you. They control your records and can delete or correct them directly in the app. See section 2 of the Privacy Policy.
