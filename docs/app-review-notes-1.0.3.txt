GUIDELINE 3.1.1 - IN-APP PURCHASE

Thank you for the clarification in your last message, it was very helpful. In this build, 1.0.3 (2), we have removed the account registration feature for businesses and organizations.

- There is no longer a sign-up link or a registration flow anywhere in the app. The screens that used to create an account and a clinic (email entry, verification code, name and password, clinic name, type and location) are not part of this build, so a person without credentials cannot create anything in it.
- The app has no purchase flow, prices, plans, payment options, or links or calls to action pointing toward a purchase. The plan endpoints the previous build used during registration are no longer called.
- One thing we would like to clarify, in case it looks like a payment feature: the payment and expense screens are a clinic's own bookkeeping. Staff note what a patient paid the clinic and what the clinic spent, after the money changed hands outside the app. Nothing is sold, unlocked or processed there, and the app never takes a payment.
- Denta is a business service sold directly to dental clinics, which we believe places it under Guideline 3.1.3(c), Enterprise Services. Our customer is the clinic rather than an individual user, and one subscription covers its staff. Clinics buy from us against an invoice and pay outside the app by bank transfer or a local method. It is not sold to consumers, families or patients, and new clinics start with a free trial we arrange directly.
- Our website describes the clinic plans. The app does not link to that page; its only external links are our privacy policy and terms of service. Purchases are arranged directly between the clinic and us.

If any screen still looks like a purchase prompt, please point us to it and we will gladly change it.
